## In one line

Pulse OS brings your whole day into one place.

## In a paragraph

Pulse OS is a personal command centre: one calm screen for your plans, the
weather, the news, the markets, your music and your travel, with an assistant
that can read all of it and act on it — typed to, or simply talked to. The
launch film shows every part of it in two and a half minutes.

## In full

Every day has a pulse, and most of us keep ours scattered across nine different
apps. Pulse OS brings it into one place. Home puts your plans, the weather and
your apps on one screen. The Launchpad opens anything on your Mac in a click.
The Life Hub keeps your Google and iCloud calendars, your to-dos and your habits
in step. Ask Pulse does what you tell it — or what you say to it out loud.
Markets & News carries live prices, local and world news and every fixture for
the teams you follow. Music brings your Spotify in, with a full-screen player
lit by the record's own colours. And Travel follows your trip from the flight to
the moment you land. Pulse OS. Your day, in a heartbeat.

## The facts

- **Pulse OS** — a personal command centre for one screen that never scrolls.
  React and Vite in front, an Express gateway behind it, and an assistant that
  runs on Gemini, OpenAI or Claude, with real-time voice over the Gemini Live API.
- **The films** — made in code with Remotion from the app's own components. The
  narration was voiced with Gemini text-to-speech, each line read three times
  with the best take chosen blind, timed word by word with whisper.cpp, and the
  picture cut to the words on a ninety-beat-a-minute grid. The ad runs at 124
  beats a minute, edited on the bars of its track, with forty-five sound effects
  set on the beats.
- **Status** — coming soon. The code is public and runs locally; see the
  [README](../../README.md).
