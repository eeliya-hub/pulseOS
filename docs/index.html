Feature17 Oct01 / 06 Projects — boards, a timeline and a table One set of cards drawn three ways: lanes for what is in flight, a timeline for when it lands, a table for the whole lot at once. Drag a card into the last list and it is ticked off.
Feature17 Oct02 / 06 Ask Pulse can work on your boards It reads a board before it answers, adds a card to the list you name, and moving one to done ticks it off. A name that fits two cards moves nothing — it asks which.
Feature17 Oct03 / 06 Schedule, rebuilt around the month The calendar is the screen now. Events are written into the days, the months either side stay in view, and the day sits beside it with what is on now lit.
Improvement17 Oct04 / 06 Mail gets a tab, and lines onto boards Asking Pulse to write is a button, not a box under every message — and highlighting a line puts a card on the right board, dated from the email’s own words.
Improvement10 Oct05 / 06 Pulse fills a big screen On a 2560 × 1440 display the layout used to sit small in the middle. It scales up to fill it now, and nothing at or below 1920 × 1080 moves.
