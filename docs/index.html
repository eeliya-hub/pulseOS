What it plugs into

It connects to the things you already use.

Sign in once with your own account — by OAuth, never a password — and Pulse reads and writes to them directly. Nothing goes through a service of ours, because there isn't one.

GmailOutlookGoogle CalendariCloud CalendarSpotifyGemini

Plus the keyless and free-tier sources behind the rest: OpenWeather, Finnhub and CoinGecko, GNews and RSS, Football‑Data, balldontlie and Jolpica, LRCLIB lyrics, OpenStreetMap and OpenFreeMap, community ADS‑B for live flights, and the ECB's own exchange rates. OpenAI and Claude can stand in for Gemini.

Home

The day, as you open it

Who you are, what is next, what the sky is doing, and the handful of apps you actually use. The background is the hour itself — dawn, day, dusk and night each light the whole screen differently, so a glance tells you where you are in the day before you have read a word.

Life Hub

Every calendar, to-do, habit and project

Google Calendar, iCloud and any .ics feed in one place — read and written both ways, so an event made here is an event on your phone. Beside them: the day's to-dos, the habits you are keeping, and the projects they belong to. And now the mail card, saying what is waiting before you go and look.

Mail

New Gmail and Outlook, read by your assistant

Connect Gmail or Outlook and your email joins the rest of your day. Pulse reads a message once and finds the meetings, the deadlines and the things you have been asked to do — then proposes each one for your calendar or your to‑do list and waits for you. It never sends, files or deletes anything on its own, and it cannot permanently delete mail at all.

GmailOutlookMicrosoft 365OAuth only

Ask Pulse

An assistant that can act, and listen

An assistant that can see everything on this page and change it. Put the call in the diary, start the playlist, add the task, draft the reply. Type it, or hold a key and say it out loud — voice mode listens, works while you watch, and answers in kind.

Travel

Flights tracked live, with no key at all

A trip with the flight tracked live from community ADS‑B receivers, the local time and weather where you are going, the exchange rate with a month of history behind it, a packing list, and a map you can plan the days on. All of it works with no paid key.

Launchpad

Every app and site, on one shelf

Every application on your machine and every site you keep going back to, on one shelf, in folders you arrange yourself. Pulse finds what you have installed, takes any icon you like, and notices new apps while it is open.
